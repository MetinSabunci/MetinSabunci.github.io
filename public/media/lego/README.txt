Put your LEGO Animation files here, then reference them in src/data/projects.js
(paths WITHOUT a leading slash), for example:

  cardVideo: 'media/lego/card.mp4'
  gallery: [{ type: 'video', src: 'media/lego/film.mp4', poster: 'media/lego/film.jpg', caption: 'Final film' }, ...]

Tips
- Card clip: short loop, 5-15 s, H.264 .mp4, no audio needed, ideally under 5 MB, 1280px wide.
- Final film: H.264 .mp4, 1080p or 720p. Very large files are better hosted on YouTube/Vimeo.
- Images: .webp or optimised .jpg, about 1600px wide.
